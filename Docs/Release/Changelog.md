# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- **A rule per freight car, per timetable stop:** Platform (vanilla), Skip, Load or Unload.
- **Skipped cars** are left alone and don't hold the train, even with "Fully load/unload".
- **Load and Unload** switch the platform beside the car for that dock only, then put the platform's own setting back.
- **Set rules in the timetable editor** (a "Freight cars at this stop" section in each stop's settings) or in a
  **freight car's menu** ("Timetable rules").
- Works with vanilla stations, **Modular Stations** and **DynamicTimeTable**.
- Multiplayer and dedicated servers (Windows and Linux).

**Good to know**

- Everyone in a multiplayer session needs Freight Car Control.
